PLAYER AGREEMENT
1. Respect everyone. This is a friendly game among friends. No threats, harassment, bullying, retaliation, or pressure about votes.
2. Private ballots. Do not demand to see another player's selections. The app shows totals only when the host reveals results.
3. One vote each. Choose exactly four other players. You cannot select yourself, and you cannot change a submitted ballot.
4. Fair play. Do not use another person's account or try to interfere with the game.
5. Host controls. The host may open or close voting and reveal the final totals. The host also participates as a player.
6. Trial notice. This version stores data on one browser/device. Anyone with access to that device may be able to use it. Do not use a password you use elsewhere.
